SECTION 1: CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION FORMATS
===================================================================================

1.1 CUSTOMER ID FORMAT (CUS-XXXXX)
Format: CUS-[5 digit numeric code]
Pattern: CUS-XXXXX where X = 0-9
Examples: CUS-20077, CUS-10001, CUS-95432
Rules:
- Generated server-side at account opening
- Unique across Meridian Financial Group
- Immutable; cannot be changed
- Used for all customer inquiries and identification
- Validation: Must match pattern /^CUS-\d{5}$/ or return TypeError: InvalidCustomerID

Acceptable Customer IDs for Showcase Scenario:
- CUS-20077 (Arjun Mehta - Primary showcase customer)
- CUS-20076 (Test customer - spouse/co-applicant scenarios)
- CUS-20075 (Test customer - cross-product inquiry)
- CUS-20074 (Test customer - error scenarios)
- CUS-20073 (Test customer - escalation scenarios)

1.2 ACCOUNT NUMBER FORMAT (ACC-XXXXX)
Format: ACC-[5 digit numeric code]
Pattern: ACC-XXXXX where X = 0-9
Examples: ACC-20077, ACC-10001, ACC-95432
Rules:
- Unique identifier for each bank account
- One customer can have multiple account numbers
- Account linking requires valid ACC and customer match
- Validation: Must match pattern /^ACC-\d{5}$/ or return TypeError: InvalidAccountID

Account Numbers for Showcase Scenario:
- ACC-20077 (Arjun Mehta savings account, linked to CUS-20077)
- ACC-20001 (Test account for business/current account scenarios)
- ACC-20002 (Test account for cross-account transfer scenarios)

1.3 POLICY NUMBER FORMAT (POL-XX-XXXXX)
Format: POL-[2 letter product code]-[5 digit numeric]
Pattern: POL-[A-Z]{2}-\d{5} where X = 0-9
Examples: 
- POL-IN-30091 (Insurance policy)
- POL-HM-10001 (Home insurance)
- POL-MO-50321 (Motor insurance)
- POL-HE-70125 (Health insurance)

Product Code Legend:
- IN = General Insurance (multi-risk)
- HM = Home Insurance
- MO = Motor Insurance
- HE = Health Insurance
- TR = Travel Insurance
- BU = Business Liability Insurance
- PL = Personal Liability Insurance
- GP = Group Insurance

Rules:
- Product code indicates insurance type; scope of coverage
- One customer can have multiple policies
- Policy number must exist in insurance_server database to access details
- Validation: Must match pattern /^POL-[A-Z]{2}-\d{5}$/ or return TypeError: InvalidPolicyID

Policy Numbers for Showcase Scenario:
- POL-IN-30091 (Arjun Mehta general insurance policy, linked to CUS-20077)
- POL-HM-20001 (Home insurance for various scenarios)
- POL-MO-50001 (Motor insurance for various scenarios)

1.4 PORTFOLIO ID FORMAT (PORT-XXXXX)
Format: PORT-[5 digit numeric code]
Pattern: PORT-\d{5} where X = 0-9
Examples: PORT-20077, PORT-10001, PORT-95432
Rules:
- Unique identifier for each wealth management portfolio
- One customer can have multiple portfolios (rare; typically one primary)
- Portfolio must be accessed through wealth_server only
- Validation: Must match pattern /^PORT-\d{5}$/ or return TypeError: InvalidPortfolioID

Portfolio IDs for Reference:
- PORT-20077 (Arjun Mehta wealth portfolio, linked to CUS-20077)
- PORT-20001 (Test portfolio for investment scenarios)

1.5 PRODUCT CODE FORMAT (PROD-XX-XX)
Format: PROD-[2 letter category]-[2 letter type]
Pattern: PROD-[A-Z]{2}-[A-Z]{2}
Examples:
- PROD-BK-SA (Banking - Savings Account)
- PROD-BK-CD (Banking - Credit Card)
- PROD-IS-HM (Insurance - Home)
- PROD-IS-MO (Insurance - Motor)
- PROD-WE-EQ (Wealth - Equity Fund)
- PROD-WE-DB (Wealth - Debt Fund)

Category Codes:
- BK = Banking
- IS = Insurance
- WE = Wealth/Investments
- LO = Loans
- CR = Credit Products

Type Codes (examples):
Banking Types: SA, CD, FD, RD, CU, LO, HL, PL, VL, EL
Insurance Types: HM, MO, HE, TR, BU, PL, GP, PE
Wealth Types: EQ, DB, BD, HB, RE, AF, GB

Rules:
- Product codes identify available products for sale/offering
- Used in portfolio construction and product recommendations
- Validation: Must match /^PROD-[A-Z]{2}-[A-Z]{2}$/ or return ValueError: InvalidProductCode

1.6 CLAIM NUMBER FORMAT (CLM-XXXXX)
Format: CLM-[5 digit numeric code]
Pattern: CLM-\d{5}
Examples: CLM-20077, CLM-10001, CLM-95432
Rules:
- Generated at claim submission
- Unique across all claims for tracking
- Links to specific policy number
- Used for claim status inquiries and communication
- Validation: Must match /^CLM-\d{5}$/ or return TypeError: InvalidClaimID

SECTION 2: PAYMENT METHODS & AUTO-DEBIT TERMS
===================================================================================

2.1 AUTO-DEBIT PAYMENT METHOD
Availability: Savings, Current, and linked accounts
Frequency Options:
- Monthly: Processed on date selected (1-28 of month)
- Quarterly: Jan-Apr-Jul-Oct, Apr-Jul-Oct-Jan, Jul-Oct-Jan-Apr, Oct-Jan-Apr-Jul
- Half-yearly: January & July, or April & October
- Annual: Single date in calendar year

Retry Logic:
- First attempt: On selected due date
- If failed (insufficient balance): Retry on next business day
- If second attempt fails: Auto-debit mandate canceled
- Notification sent to customer on both failures

Amount Flexibility:
- Fixed amount: Same amount each period (e.g., EMI of 5,000 INR)
- Variable amount: Varies based on outstanding balance (e.g., insurance premium)
- Minimum transaction: 100 INR per auto-debit
- Maximum per transaction: 10,00,000 INR (subject to daily limit)

Authorization Requirements:
- Physical mandate form (signed by customer) OR
- E-mandate via online portal (e-signed with Aadhaar)
- Mandate valid for 24 months; automatic renewal if not canceled
- Cancellation notice: 5 business days prior to next scheduled debit

Use Cases:
- Insurance premium payment (all insurance types)
- Loan EMI (housing, personal, auto, education loans)
- Credit card bill payment (full or minimum amount)
- Utility bills and subscriptions (via linked providers)
- Investment (SIP - Systematic Investment Plan)
- Savings plan (recurring deposit contributions)

2.2 ONLINE TRANSFER METHODS

NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer):
- Settlement time: Real-time (within minutes during business hours)
- Business hours: 8 AM - 7 PM on weekdays, 8 AM - 1 PM on Saturdays
- Charges: 2.5 INR per transaction for amounts <100,000 INR (flat rate)
- Useful for: Regular transfers, quick settlements
- Minimum amount: 1 INR
- Maximum amount: 10,00,000 INR per transaction

RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement):
- Settlement time: Real-time (typically 30 minutes)
- Business hours: 9 AM - 4:30 PM on weekdays (closed Saturdays)
- Charges: 25 INR per transaction (flat rate, irrespective of amount)
- Useful for: Urgent, large-value transfers
- Minimum amount: 2,00,000 INR
- Maximum amount: 1 crore INR per transaction

IMPS (Immediate Payment Service):
- Settlement time: 24/7 instant settlement
- Available: Round-the-clock, including weekends & holidays
- Charges: 5 INR per transaction
- Useful for: Urgent payments at any time
- Minimum amount: 1 INR
- Maximum amount: 10,00,000 INR per transaction

2.3 PAYMENT CHANNELS & VERIFICATION

Branch Counter Payment:
- Accepted: Cash, cheque, draft, credit card (EMI option)
- Documents: Passbook/account statement + identity proof
- Processing time: Immediate (cash); 3-5 days (cheque)
- Charges: Nil for cash/cheque; GST applicable on credit card surcharge

Mobile App / Online Portal:
- Available: 24/7 for NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, auto-debit setup
- Authentication: Multi-factor (password + OTP)
- Limits: Verification required for first transfer to new beneficiary
- Processing time: Immediate to real-time depending on method

ATM Withdrawal:
- Daily limit: 1,00,000 INR (Standard tier); Unlimited (Premium tier)
- Charges: Free withdrawals (Standard 5/month), additional 20 INR each
- Account access: Only from Meridian ATMs or partner network

Call Center / Phone Banking:
- Available: 9 AM - 9 PM weekdays; 10 AM - 6 PM Saturdays
- Services: Bill pay, fund transfer (pre-registered beneficiaries only), status queries
- Verification: Security questions, OTP confirmation
- Toll-free: 1800-121-2020

2.4 PAYMENT FAILURE & DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Failure Scenarios & Resolution:

Insufficient Balance:
- Status: Transaction rejected; amount not debited
- Notification: SMS/email within 1 hour
- Retry: System retries once on next business day (auto-debit only)
- Resolution: Top-up account balance and retry

Invalid Account/Beneficiary:
- Status: Bounce; transaction returned to source account
- Timeline: Return within 3-5 business days
- Charges: Bounce charge of 200-500 INR applied by receiving bank
- Prevention: Verify account details before initiating transfer

Duplicate Transactions:
- Status: Two identical transactions appear in account
- Claim: Report within 30 days of discovery
- Investigation: Bank investigates if duplicate charge occurred
- Reversal: If confirmed, duplicate amount credited within 30 days

Partial Payment/Amount Mismatch:
- Status: Amount received differs from amount sent
- Investigation: Bank traces with receiving bank (3-7 days)
- Resolution: If no trace found in time, dispute escalated to ombudsman
- Timeline: 45 days from discovery

Transfer to Wrong Beneficiary:
- Status: Money transferred to unintended recipient
- Prevention: Verify account name/IFSC before transfer
- Recovery: Impossible if beneficiary cashes out; legal recourse only
- Mitigation: Banks now verify account name (matches first 4-6 characters)

2.5 REGULATORY & LIMIT RULES

Daily Transaction Limits:
- ATM withdrawal: 1,00,000 INR (Standard tier; Premium unlimited)
- Online transfer: No daily limit (but individual transaction caps apply)
- Card transaction: 5,00,000 INR (varies by card tier)
- Total debit per day: No regulatory limit (subject to account balance)

Monthly Transaction Limits:
- ATM withdrawals: 5 free; additional at 20 INR each
- NEFT/RTGS transfers: Unlimited
- Cheque issuance: Unlimited
- Check book replenishment: One book (20-25 leaves) per request

Verification Requirements:
- First transfer to new beneficiary: Requires 24-48 hour verification
- Large transactions (>10 lakhs): May trigger additional verification
- International transfers: Require FEMA compliance documentation
- Remittance beyond 50,000 INR per financial year: Tax filing requirement

SECTION 3: PAYMENT STATUS & RECONCILIATION
===================================================================================

3.1 TRANSACTION STATUS CODES

Pending: Payment initiated; awaiting bank processing
- Typical duration: 1-24 hours for online transfers
- Action: None; wait for processing

Processing: Bank is processing the transaction
- Typical duration: Real-time to 2-3 business days
- Action: Monitor account; contact support if delayed >3 days

Completed/Success: Payment successfully debited and credited
- Money available in recipient's account immediately (RTGS/IMPS) or within 24 hours (NEFT)
- Confirmation email/SMS sent to both parties
- No action required; reconcile in accounting if needed

Failed: Payment rejected/returned
- Reason: Insufficient balance, invalid account, closed account, etc.
- Amount: Refunded to source account within 1-3 business days
- Charges: Bounce charge may apply
- Action: Identify reason and retry with corrected details

On Hold: Payment held pending verification
- Reason: Duplicate check, fraud check, customer verification pending
- Duration: 2-7 business days
- Action: Respond to verification requests if contacted

Reversed: Completed payment recalled
- Reason: Regulatory requirement, fraud detection, customer request
- Timeline: 5-30 days after original transaction
- Action: Contact support if reversal seems incorrect

3.2 PAYMENT RECONCILIATION FOR INVOICES

Auto-Debit Confirmation:
- Confirmation SMS: Sent within 5 minutes of successful debit
- Bank statement: Debit appears same day (online portal, next calendar day for passbook)
- Reference number: Provided in SMS for traceability

Manual Payment Confirmation:
- Receipt generated: Immediately after cash/cheque submission
- Processing notification: Email/SMS when cheque clears (3-5 business days)
- Credited confirmation: When amount posts to payee account

Invoice Matching:
- Standard practice: Match transaction amount to invoice amount ±
- Tolerance: Exact match required for auto-debit; manual review if variance >500 INR
- Outstanding balance: Updated in system within 24 hours of successful payment

SECTION 4: PAYMENT SCENARIO MATRIX FOR NEXUS
===================================================================================

Common Payment Scenarios & Recommended Methods:

Insurance Premium Payment:
- Method: Auto-debit (preferred), online transfer, cheque, branch counter
- Frequency: Annual, half-yearly, or monthly
- Best for: Auto-debit avoids missed payments
- Timeline: Due on stated date; 15-day grace period before lapse

Loan EMI Payment:
- Method: Auto-debit (strongly recommended), online transfer, cheque
- Frequency: Monthly (standard)
- Best for: Auto-debit ensures never-miss discipline
- Penalty: Late payment fee if >5 days late

Credit Card Bill Payment:
- Method: Auto-debit (full bill), online transfer, branch counter
- Frequency: Monthly (statement due date)
- Best for: Auto-debit for full bill (avoids interest)
- Grace period: 45 days interest-free if paid in full

Utility & Subscription Bills:
- Method: Auto-debit (preferred), online portal, branch counter
- Frequency: Monthly/quarterly/annual
- Best for: Auto-debit prevents service disconnection
- Reminder: Notify issuer for auto-debit authorization first

Investment Contributions (SIP):
- Method: Auto-debit (standard), lump-sum online transfer
- Frequency: Monthly/quarterly/annual
- Best for: Auto-debit ensures disciplined investing
- Minimum: 500 INR per month typical minimum

Cross-Product Payments:
- Example: Insurance premium from banking account; portfolio investment withdrawal to savings
- Method: Depends on source and destination products
- Coordination: Nexus orchestrates source account via banking_server → destination via insurance/wealth_server
- Error handling: If source account restrictions detected, escalation required

SECTION 5: PAYMENT ERROR HANDLING FOR NEXUS
===================================================================================

Error Scenarios & Nexus Responses:

Invalid Customer ID (CUS-XXXXX mismatch):
- Error Type: TypeError
- Nexus Action: Return typed error at intake layer; no retrieval/payment attempt
- Customer Message: "Customer ID format invalid or not found. Please verify your customer ID (format: CUS-XXXXX)"
- Example: CUS-2077 (missing zero) → rejection

Invalid Account ID (ACC-XXXXX mismatch):
- Error Type: ValueError  
- Nexus Action: Surface error to user; no payment attempt
- Customer Message: "Account {account_id} not found or invalid. Please verify account number."
- Example: AC-20077 (missing C) → rejection

Insufficient Balance:
- Error Type: InsufficientFundsError
- Nexus Action: Return error; no debit attempted
- Customer Message: "Insufficient balance in account {masked_account}. Required: ₹{amount}, Available: ₹{balance}"
- Resolution: Customer tops up account and retries

Account Restriction/Frozen:
- Error Type: AccountRestrictedError
- Nexus Action: Surface error; escalate to human
- Customer Message: "Account {masked_account} is temporarily restricted. Please contact support at 1800-121-2020."
- Reason: KYC overdue, fraud flag, court order, etc.

Payment Method Unavailable:
- Error Type: PaymentMethodUnavailableError
- Nexus Action: Suggest alternative method; no payment attempt
- Customer Message: "RTGS unavailable after 4:30 PM. Use IMPS (instant) or NEFT tomorrow."
- Timing: RTGS unavailable after 4:30 PM, all day Saturday

Duplicate Payment Detection:
- Error Type: DuplicatePaymentWarning  
- Nexus Action: Alert user; require confirmation before proceeding
- Customer Message: "Similar payment just processed 1 hour ago. Proceed with new payment? Reply YES/NO."
- Prevention: Reduces accidental duplicate payments
