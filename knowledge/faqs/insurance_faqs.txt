MERIDIAN FINANCIAL GROUP - INSURANCE PRODUCTS FAQs
Last Updated: 2026-01-01

SECTION 1: HOME INSURANCE

Q1.1: What does Meridian Home Shield cover?
A: Meridian Home Shield provides coverage for:
- Structure damage from fire, lightning, explosion, riot
- Theft, burglary, and housebreaking (if entry signs are visible)
- Accidental damage to electrical and plumbing installations
- Personal liability (up to 25 lakhs in Premium plan)
- Contents and movables (furniture, appliances, valuables up to sub-limit)

Q1.2: What is NOT covered under home insurance?
A: Standard exclusions include:
- War, civil unrest, acts of terrorism (unless optional rider purchased)
- Damage from customer's intentional acts or gross negligence
- Structural defects known at property purchase
- Damage if property is unoccupied for more than 90 consecutive days
- Flood and water damage (unless optional rider purchased)
- Wear and tear or damage from natural deterioration

Q1.3: What is the deductible, and how does it affect my claim?
A: Deductible is the amount you pay when filing a claim; the insurer pays the remaining amount. For Home Shield Basic, the deductible is 50,000 INR per claim. If you have damage of 2 lakhs INR, you pay 50,000 INR, and Meridian pays 1,50,000 INR.

Q1.4: How do I file a home insurance claim?
A: Steps to file a claim:
1. Notify Meridian within 14 days of discovering the loss
2. Submit claim form with supporting documents (photos, receipts, police FIR if theft)
3. Loss assessor inspects the property within 7 days
4. Assessment report submitted within 30 days
5. Claim settled within 30 days of approval

Q1.5: Can I add optional riders to my home policy?
A: Yes, optional riders available include:
- Earthquake coverage: Adds 15% to base premium
- Flood coverage: Adds 8% to base premium
- Home care benefits: Adds 5% to base premium (emergency plumbing, electrical repairs)
- Jewelry and valuables: Sub-limit increase (requires separate appraisal)

Q1.6: What if I want to cancel my home insurance policy mid-term?
A: You can cancel anytime with 30 days' written notice. Pro-rata refund is calculated and issued within 15 days. If the policy is cancelled by Meridian for non-compliance, 60-day notice is provided with full pro-rata refund.

SECTION 2: MOTOR INSURANCE

Q2.1: Is motor insurance mandatory in India?
A: Yes, third-party motor insurance is mandatory under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. The minimum coverage is 1 lakh INR for two-wheelers and 1 lakh INR for four-wheelers. Own-damage (comprehensive) coverage is optional but highly recommended.

Q2.2: What is the difference between Third-Party and Comprehensive coverage?
A: Third-Party coverage only covers damage you cause to another person's vehicle/property. You pay for your own vehicle damage. Comprehensive coverage includes your own damage, theft, and natural calamities.

Q2.3: What is a No-Claim Bonus (NCB), and how does it work?
A: NCB is a discount on premium given if you don't file any claim in a policy year. The discount increases each claim-free year (Year 1: 20%, Year 2: 25%, Year 3: 40%, etc.). If you file a claim, the NCB is reset to zero.

Q2.4: Can I transfer my existing no-claim bonus to a new insurer?
A: Yes, you can transfer your NCB to any new insurer by submitting the previous policy and NCB certificate. The new insurer is legally required to honor your NCB history.

Q2.5: What is the claim settlement process for motor insurance?
A: Claim process:
1. Notify insurer within 48 hours and file claim form
2. File police report (for theft/accident with injury)
3. Vehicle inspection within 3-5 days
4. Repairing garage finalized (cashless or reimbursement basis)
5. Settlement within 14 days of approval (cashless) or 30 days (reimbursement)

Q2.6: Are there any exclusions in motor insurance claims?
A: Common exclusions include:
- Depreciation on parts >3 years old (charged at 50% of claim)
- Damage from racing or stunt driving
- Damage due to mechanical breakdown or poor maintenance
- Damage if third-party is not available at accident site
- Wear and tear of tyres, batteries, oils

Q2.7: What is cashless claim in motor insurance?
A: Cashless claim means Meridian directly settles the repair cost with the authorized repairing garage, and you pay only the deductible. No cash outlay or reimbursement required. Meridian has tie-ups with over 500 garages nationally.

SECTION 3: HEALTH INSURANCE

Q3.1: What is covered under Meridian Health Insurance?
A: Coverage includes:
- Hospitalization expenses (room rent, medicines, doctor fees)
- Day-care procedures (surgery and diagnostic under 24-hour stay)
- Pre-hospitalization expenses (30 days before admission)
- Post-hospitalization expenses (60 days after discharge)
- Ambulance charges (up to 1,000 INR per claim)
- Maternity cover (after 9-month waiting period)

Q3.2: What is the waiting period for pre-existing conditions?
A: Pre-existing diseases have a 30-day waiting period for symptomatic coverage. However, some insurers may impose longer waiting periods (up to 12 months) for specific conditions. Please check your policy document for details.

Q3.3: What is Cashless Hospitalization?
A: Cashless hospitalization allows you to get treated at a network hospital without paying upfront. Meridian settles the bill directly with the hospital. You only pay the deductible and any amount exceeding the policy limit.

Q3.4: How do I claim reimbursement for medical expenses?
A: Steps for reimbursement claims:
1. Submit original medical bills and receipts
2. Submit claim form within 30 days of discharge
3. Provide hospitalization proof (discharge summary, medical reports)
4. Claim reviewed and approved within 15 days
5. Reimbursement processed within 30 days of approval

Q3.5: What is the claim process if I'm hospitalized unexpectedly?
A: In emergency situations:
1. Call Meridian immediately with policy details
2. Get admitted to a network hospital (or non-network if emergency)
3. Inform hospital staff you have Meridian insurance
4. Hospital coordinates with Meridian for authorization
5. During discharge, authorize only the required amount
6. If non-network, submit bills for reimbursement later

Q3.6: Are routine check-ups and vaccinations covered?
A: No, routine check-ups, vaccinations, and preventive screenings are not covered under standard health insurance. However, some premium plans include annual health check-up benefits worth 5,000-10,000 INR.

Q3.7: What happens if I exceed the policy limit in a year?
A: Once you exhaust your annual policy limit, any further medical expenses are your responsibility. You must renew the policy for the next year to get coverage again. Some policies offer carryover of unused balance to next year (up to 50% of limit).

SECTION 4: TRAVEL INSURANCE

Q4.1: What is covered under Meridian Travel Insurance?
A: Coverage includes:
- Trip cancellation (up to 50% of trip cost)
- Medical emergencies abroad (up to 5 lakhs)
- Baggage loss or delay (up to 2,00,000 INR)
- Flight delay compensation (300 INR per hour after 6 hours delay)
- Emergency evacuation and repatriation
- Personal accident and disability coverage
- 24/7 global emergency helpline

Q4.2: What activities are NOT covered under travel insurance?
A: Exclusions typically include:
- High-risk activities: Mountaineering, rock climbing, skiing
- Professional sports or adventure sports
- Travel to countries under government travel warnings
- Claims arising from traveling while unwell or with known medical conditions (undisclosed)
- Travel with criminal intent or for unlawful activities
- Claims filed after the 90-day limit from trip end

Q4.3: When should I purchase travel insurance?
A: It's recommended to purchase travel insurance within 14 days of your initial trip booking. If purchased later, coverage for pre-existing conditions and trip cancellation may be limited or excluded. Purchase before final payment of trip to maximize coverage.

Q4.4: What is covered if my flight is delayed?
A: Meridian Travel Insurance covers flight delay compensation of 300 INR per hour (payable after 6 hours of delay). Minimum payout: 1,800 INR (for 6-hour delay). Maximum payout: 3,000 INR per claim. Proof of delay (boarding pass, airline confirmation) is required.

Q4.5: How do I claim if my baggage is lost or delayed?
A: For lost baggage:
1. File a report with airline immediately and get written report
2. Submit claim to Meridian with airline report and photo of baggage tags
3. Claim is settled within 15 days

For delayed baggage:
1. Keep receipts of emergency purchases (clothes, toiletries)
2. Submit receipts with claim form
3. Reimbursement up to 10,000 INR per 12-hour delay (max 30,000 INR per trip)

Q4.6: Can I purchase travel insurance for trips already in progress?
A: No, travel insurance cannot be purchased after the trip has commenced. The trip start date must be in the future (at least 1-7 days depending on plan). Pre-purchase is mandatory for coverage to apply.

SECTION 5: GENERAL INSURANCE QUESTIONS

Q5.1: What is an insurance premium, and how is it calculated?
A: Premium is the amount you pay to purchase insurance coverage. It's calculated based on:
- Risk profile (age, health, driving record)
- Coverage amount and type
- Claim history (no-claim bonus)
- Geographic location
- Coverage period (annual, half-yearly, monthly)

Q5.2: What is the policy renewal process?
A: Renewal happens automatically 30 days before expiry. You receive a renewal notice with updated premium. If you authorize the payment (via auto-debit or manual payment), the policy is renewed. Renewal preserves your no-claim bonus and claim history.

Q5.3: What documents do I need during claim investigation?
A: Standard documents include:
- Original policy document
- Claim form (signed)
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN)
- Medical reports or police FIR (if applicable)
- Original invoices and receipts
- Photos of damage/loss
- Post-incident reports or certificates
- Any third-party documents (accident report, liability admission)

Q5.4: Can I have multiple insurance policies with different insurers?
A: Yes, you can have multiple policies. However, insurance operates on the indemnity principle—you cannot claim more than your actual loss even with multiple policies. You must disclose existing coverage to avoid issues during claims.

Q5.5: What should I do if my claim is rejected?
A: If your claim is rejected:
1. Request detailed rejection reason in writing
2. Review policy terms to understand grounds for rejection
3. File an appeal within 30 days with additional supporting documents
4. If unsatisfied, escalate to Meridian Grievance Officer
5. After 30 days, file complaint with Insurance Ombudsman (free service)
